# Client assets needed

Nothing on the live site uses stock photography — every photo slot either shows a real photo or
the generated fallback Docket art (mono technical-drawing pattern, no substitute imagery). This
list is what unlocks the photo slots.

## Domestic photography

- 8–12 completed jobs, **before AND after from the same standing position** — this is the
  detail most tradespeople skip, and the one that makes the before/after slider work
- Detail shots of neat pipework and finished installs

## Commercial photography

- Plant rooms
- Commercial boiler installations
- Site work in **correct, complete PPE** — hard hat, hi-vis, boots, gloves where appropriate.
  A missing hard hat in a commercial shot reads as a safety-culture failure to a procurement
  buyer and will not be used.
- Van on a commercial site
- Pipework runs
- A director on site with a tablet/clipboard

## Portraits

- Both directors individually — workwear, daylight, plain background or against the van
- One photo of both together on site

## Shooting rules

- Landscape for hero use, portrait for detail shots
- Daylight only, wipe the lens first
- Step closer instead of zooming digitally
- PPE correct in every single commercial frame, no exceptions

## Documents

- [ ] Gas Safe registration certificates (both directors)
- [ ] All current insurance certificates (public liability, employers' liability, professional
      indemnity, contract works)
- [ ] Any accreditation certificates already held
- [ ] Manufacturer accreditation certificates
- [ ] Existing logo files, if any exist (vector/SVG preferred)
- [ ] Any existing written policies (H&S, environmental, quality, equality, modern slavery)

## Written content (voice notes are fine — happy to transcribe)

- [ ] How the two directors met and why they went 50/50
- [ ] Six jobs they're proud of, with real scope, for `/projects` once it's built
- [ ] Honest domestic pricing for the jobs listed in `CONTENT_NEEDED.md`
- [ ] Housing stock and commercial profile of each covered area (this is the SEO moat described
      in §8.8 of the brief — a one-conversation extraction, not guesswork)
- [ ] What they refuse to do
- [ ] Any commercial work already completed, with scope and duration, and whether the client
      will permit their name being used
